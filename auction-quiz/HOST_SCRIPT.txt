========================================================
BID FOR C — HOST / ADMIN SCRIPT
========================================================
Use this as a speaking guide, not a script to read word-for-word.
Adjust energy and pacing to the room. Bracketed notes [ ] are cues
for you, not lines to say out loud.

--------------------------------------------------------
1. PRE-EVENT / WELCOME (before registration closes)
--------------------------------------------------------
"Good [morning/afternoon] everyone, and welcome to BID FOR C!

Today isn't your usual quiz. There are no static teams, no fixed
turns, and no one just waiting around. Every question up for grabs
is going to be auctioned — and the loudest bidder doesn't win, the
smartest one does.

If you haven't registered yet, scan the QR code / go to the link on
screen right now. Enter your two player names, your email, and your
phone number. Your team name will be assigned automatically, so no
time wasted arguing about that.

Every team starts with 1000 coins. Coins are your bidding power —
spend them to win the right to answer a question. Reward points are
what actually get you on the leaderboard. Remember that distinction,
because it matters a lot later: coins get you IN the game, points win
you the game.

You've got [X] minutes to register. Let's move fast."

[Wait for registrations, watch the admin panel team count]

--------------------------------------------------------
2. RULES BRIEFING (once registration closes)
--------------------------------------------------------
"Alright, registration is closed. Let me walk you through exactly how
this works, because I will not repeat this later.

One. A question appears on the big screen. You won't see the full
difficulty or content until the auction for it opens.

Two. When I start the auction, every team can place a bid using their
coins. Bids go up in increments of 20 or 50 coins — you'll see the
buttons on your screen. The clock is ticking, so don't sit on it.

Three. When the timer runs out, the highest bidder wins the auction —
and only that team gets to attempt the question.

Four. Once you win, the actual challenge unlocks and your solve timer
starts immediately. Solve it correctly within the time limit, and you
earn reward points. Fail, or run out of time, and you get nothing —
but your coins are already spent, so bid carefully.

Five. Coins you spend bidding do NOT come back if you lose the
question. So don't blow your whole balance on the first round because
it looked exciting.

Six. Difficulty goes up as we go — Super Easy, Easy, Medium, Hard,
Bonus rounds, Code Fix, Code Completion, and yes — a Jackpot round
near the end that can flip the entire leaderboard. Save some coins for
that.

The scoreboard is live and visible throughout, so everyone will always
know exactly where they stand.

Any questions? [pause] Good. Let's begin."

--------------------------------------------------------
3. STARTING THE FIRST AUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------
"Here we go — first question of the day!

[Trigger: show question category on screen]

This one's a [Super Easy / Easy / Medium / Hard] question, worth
[reward] points, with a [time] second solve window once someone wins
it.

Auction... starts... NOW."

[Start auction on admin panel]

--------------------------------------------------------
4. DURING BIDDING (energy lines / filler while timer runs)
--------------------------------------------------------
Use these as needed while a bidding timer is active — read the room,
don't force every line.

"Coins are moving — who wants this one?"
"[Team name] just took the lead with [amount] coins!"
"Ten seconds left — last call on the bidding!"
"Careful — someone's about to run out of coins to compete with."
"This is a cheap one, why is nobody bidding?"
"Big spenders in the back — let's see who really wants it."

--------------------------------------------------------
5. AUCTION WON — HANDOFF TO THE TASK
--------------------------------------------------------
"And the auction goes to [Team Name] for [final bid] coins!

[Team Name], the challenge is live on your screen right now. Your
timer starts... now. You have [time_limit] seconds. Everyone else,
watch the big screen — let's see if they can pull it off."

[Start task timer]

--------------------------------------------------------
6. RESULT DECLARATION
--------------------------------------------------------
PASS:
"Time's up — and it's a PASS! [Team Name] solves it and earns
[reward points] points. Excellent work. Scoreboard is updating right
now — check where you stand."

FAIL:
"And... that's a fail. Tough luck, [Team Name] — the coins are spent,
but there's no reward this time. Chin up, there's plenty of auction
left."

FAIL WITH FALLBACK (if used):
"[Team Name] couldn't crack it in time — but the challenge isn't dead
yet. [Fallback Team], you're up. Let's see if you can finish what they
started."

--------------------------------------------------------
7. TRANSITIONING BETWEEN DIFFICULTY TIERS
--------------------------------------------------------
"That wraps up our [Super Easy / Easy] questions. From here on,
things get tougher — and more expensive. [Medium] tier starts now,
so budget your coins accordingly."

[For Bonus round]
"Time for a Bonus round! These questions carry extra reward points,
so if you've got coins saved up, now's the moment to spend them."

[For Code Fix / Code Completion rounds]
"This next set is Code Fix / Code Completion — you'll be handed
broken or incomplete code and have to make it work. Read carefully
before you bid blind."

[For Jackpot round]
"This is it — the JACKPOT round. Biggest reward points on the board,
and this can completely change who's sitting at the top of the
leaderboard. Whoever wins this auction better be ready to deliver."

--------------------------------------------------------
8. MID-EVENT BREAK (if scheduled)
--------------------------------------------------------
"Let's pause here for a quick [X]-minute break. Grab some water,
stretch out — the scoreboard isn't going anywhere, and neither is the
competition. We resume at [time]."

[Resuming]
"Alright, break's over — everyone back in your seats. Let's pick up
right where we left off. Scoreboard's on screen if you need a
reminder of where you stand."

--------------------------------------------------------
9. FINAL ROUND / LAST QUESTION
--------------------------------------------------------
"This is the final question of the day. Whatever happens in the next
few minutes could decide the entire event, so let's make it count.

Auction... starts... now."

--------------------------------------------------------
10. SCOREBOARD REVEAL / RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------
"That's the last question done — incredible effort from every single
team today. Before we reveal the final standings, let's have one
round of applause for everyone who put their coins, and their code,
on the line.

[pause for applause]

Alright... let's bring up the final leaderboard."

[Trigger reveal on scoreboard screen]

"In [X]rd place... [Team Name], with [reward points] points!
In [X]nd place... [Team Name]!
And taking the WIN today, with [reward points] points... [Team
Name]! Congratulations!"

--------------------------------------------------------
11. PRIZE DISTRIBUTION (if applicable)
--------------------------------------------------------
"Can our winning teams please come up to the front? Let's get these
prizes handed out.

[Call each placing team up individually, hand over prizes/certificates]

Let's give them one more round of applause."

--------------------------------------------------------
12. CLOSING REMARKS
--------------------------------------------------------
"That's a wrap on Bid for C! Thank you to every team that registered,
every team that bid a little too aggressively, and everyone who
stuck it out till the very end.

A huge thanks to [organizing team / club / sponsors] for making this
possible, and to everyone helping run the show behind the scenes.

We hope you had as much fun playing as we did building this. Go grab
some food, catch up with your teammates, and we'll see you at the
next event. Thank you, and good night!"

--------------------------------------------------------
QUICK-REFERENCE CHEAT SHEET
--------------------------------------------------------
- Starting coins per team: 1000
- Bid increments: +20 or +50 coins
- Coins spent on a lost/failed auction are NOT refunded
- Difficulty ladder: Super Easy -> Easy -> Medium -> Hard -> Bonus ->
  Code Fix -> Code Completion -> Jackpot
- Coins = bidding power. Reward points = leaderboard score.
- Pass = reward points awarded. Fail = coins lost, no points.
